STAGE 5 — ONE-TIME USER DECISION

Under each new signal, the user sees:
- ✅ Принимаю сигнал
- ❌ Отклоняю сигнал

After either choice:
- the decision is saved in signals.db;
- both decision buttons disappear;
- copy, OKX and TradingView buttons remain;
- later TP/SL message updates do not restore the decision buttons;
- only the first delivered recipient can make a choice for their own signal message.
